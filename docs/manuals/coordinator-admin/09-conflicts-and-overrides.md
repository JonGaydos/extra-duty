# Conflicts and overrides on time edits

When an edit moves a detail's time, Signal 90 checks every deputy on it again for overlapping details and hour caps.

1. Change the times in **Quick edit**, **Edit detail** or a bulk edit, and save.
2. If a deputy would be double-booked or over a cap, nothing is saved. The panel says **This change double-books or overloads a deputy** and lists each conflict: who, and which rule.
3. Resolve it: change the times again, or remove the deputy from one of the details.
4. Or override it: type a **Reason for the override** and choose **Save with override**. The reason is kept in the audit log with the deputies and conflicts.

Assigning works the same way. If a placement breaks a rule, the form lists **This placement breaks these rules**, and you must tick **Assign anyway: I confirm overriding these rules** and give an **Override reason**. A vendor's restriction on a deputy cannot be overridden.

> Only coordinators and admins can place a deputy into a conflict. Deputies are never allowed to.
